# VidoPro

> Screen recording and video editing that makes polished videos automatically.

**Website:** [vidopro.asia](https://www.vidopro.asia)

VidoPro is a screen recording and video editing app for macOS and Windows. It zooms in on your clicks and keystrokes automatically, so you get professional screen-capture videos without manual editing in tools like Premiere or CapCut.

## Who it's for

- Content creators
- Teachers and course makers
- Programmers and product teams recording demos and tutorials

## Key features

- **Auto-zoom** on mouse clicks and keyboard actions
- **Smooth cursor** movement
- **4K 60FPS** export
- **Polished visuals** with rounded corners and 3D shadows
- **Circular webcam overlay**
- **AI subtitles** powered by Whisper (Vietnamese supported)
- Available for **macOS** and **Windows**
